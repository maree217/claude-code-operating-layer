# Company brain — Specimen Components Ltd (fictional specimen)

**Purpose:** who we are, what we know, what we've learned.

## Who we are

- Mid-sized automotive parts manufacturer, ~340 staff, one UK plant
- Sell precision-machined components to Tier 1 automotive suppliers

## Who we sell to / who we serve

- 40 active supplier/customer accounts, mostly Tier 1 automotive
- Sales Ops (3 people) handle inbound supplier queries

## Never

- Never commit customer specification data to a prototype outside the
  approved data-handling zone
- Never quote a price without Finance sign-off above £50k

## Hard-won lessons

- Lesson (Mar): quotes over £50k need finance sign-off — a verbal quote from
  a machine-shop lead nearly went out uncosted
- Lesson (Jun): supplier query response time is the #1 complaint in customer
  reviews, ahead of price

## Glossary

- **PPAP** — Production Part Approval Process, a supplier-qualification
  document set our customers require before we ship a new part
- **Tier 1** — a supplier that sells directly to a vehicle manufacturer
